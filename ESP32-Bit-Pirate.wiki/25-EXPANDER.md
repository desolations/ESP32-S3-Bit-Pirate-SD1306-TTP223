The **EXPANDER mode** allows the Bit Pirate to communicate with an external **ESP32 Bus Expander** device.


The expander acts as a **radio coprocessor**, extending the capabilities of the main Bit Pirate firmware, notably by **adding 5 GHz Wi-Fi and Zigbee support**.






## ⚡ Flash it


Use the [ESP32 Bit Pirate WebFlasher](https://geo-tp.github.io/ESP32-Bit-Pirate/webflasher/) and select **ESP32 Bus Expander (ESP32-C5, at least 4MB flash, no PSRAM needed)**.

Once it's done, you can connect the ESP32 Bus Expander **(RX9/TX10)** to the ESP32 Bit Pirate.

- [Bridge the ESP32-C5 Expander](https://geo-tp.github.io/ESP32-Bit-Pirate/recipes/bridge-esp32-c5-expander/)
